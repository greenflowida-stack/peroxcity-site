Why a voice agent

You call between jobs. We pick up between jobs too.

Pros don't buy chemical at a desk. You call from the truck, at 6 AM or after the last rinse. The agent answers right away, knows the product sheet, and never makes you repeat yourself to the next person.

Two paths.

Early in every call it asks one plain question: ready to order, or still deciding? Then it either writes up your order or learns what you need and hands you to our team.

How it works

Not a phone tree. An agent that does the work.

The agent listens, decides what to do next, and uses real tools. It saves your details to our CRM, prices your order from the price sheet and books a follow-up with a person on our team.

01 · ASK

Ask anything first

Mix ratios, Part B dosing, what it does on tile roofs, how it compares to bleach (sodium hypochlorite). Straight answers from the same sheet our technicians use.

02 · READY

Say what you need. The agent quotes kit tier pricing ($99, $89 each for two, $79 each for three or more), confirms your ship-to address and reads the order back.

Get qualified, not pushed

Not ready? It asks what kind of work you do, how many rigs, what you use now and roughly how much, and when you might switch. One question at a time.

05 · HAND-OFF

06 · HONEST

It quotes only from the price sheet. It says what PeroXcity actually does: lifts mold and mildew stains, cleans with oxygen and breaks down to water and oxygen. It's a cleaner, not a disinfectant.

FAQ

Am I talking to a person?

No. The agent is an AI, and it'll tell you so if you ask. It hands you to a person on our sales team whenever you want one, and for anything it can't settle on the call: bulk pricing, special shipping, account questions.

Do I pay on the call?

No. The agent never asks for card details. It writes up your order, and someone from PeroXcity texts and emails you a secure checkout link. The order ships once it's paid.

What can I order by voice?

Concentrate Kits ($99 for one, $89 each for two, $79 each for three or more; a box holds three) and Ready-To-Use 7% (1 gallon $29, 5 gallon $129). Free shipping on orders over $1,000. Drums and totes are priced by quote, so the agent passes those to our team.

What happens to what I tell it?

It goes into PeroXcity's sales CRM so our team can follow up without asking you everything again. We use it to help with your order and your questions, nothing else.

Where do you ship?

UPS Ground from Fort Myers, Florida, to the lower 48 states. No Alaska or Hawaii.
